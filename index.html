Why Footy Status

One connected digital home for the soccer community

Footy Status is designed to create one connected digital home for the soccer community. It allows players to showcase their development, teams to organize their presence, coaches to connect with their squads, parents to follow their children, scouts to discover talent, referees to build professional profiles, and leagues to share fixtures and results.

Footy Status is built to make soccer profiles, highlights, team connections, fixtures, results, and player discovery easier and more accessible — for everyone in the game.

PlayersShowcase development, highlights, and stats.
Teams & AcademiesOrganize rosters, staff, and presence.
Coaches & ParentsStay connected to squads and children.
Scouts & RefereesDiscover talent and build professional profiles.
Getting started

How to Use Footy Status

Five simple steps to build your presence in the Footy Status community.

1

Create Your Account

Choose the account type that matches your role in the soccer community.

PlayerParentCoach or StaffTeam or AcademyScoutReferee
2

Build Your Profile

Add your name, profile picture, biography, team information, playing position, experience, contact information, and other details that apply to your account type.

3

Connect With Your Soccer Community

Players can connect with their teams. Parents can link with their children. Coaches and team staff can connect with their clubs or school teams. Search for players, teams, leagues, referees, coaches, and scouts.

4

Share and Discover Highlights

Players can upload short soccer highlights to the Next Up feed. Other users can discover players, watch clips, interact with content, and follow emerging talent.

5

Follow Matches and Results

View fixtures, match details, leagues, teams, recent results, and other important soccer information directly inside the app.
